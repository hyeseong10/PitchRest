# ⚽ PitchRest: Athlete Sleep & Performance Tracker

## 📌 Overview
**PitchRest** is an interactive web application designed for soccer players to track their sleep patterns and analyze their recovery status. Unlike generic sleep trackers, PitchRest is **built on academic research**, providing science-based insights into how sleep affects athletic performance and injury risk.

## 🔍 Research Background
This project originated from an in-depth academic study on the correlation between sleep and soccer performance. Key research integrated into the app's logic includes:
- **Sleep & Performance:** Investigating how sleep deprivation impacts reaction time, decision-making, and motor control (based on Halson's research).
- **Injury Risk & Recovery:** Analyzing the relationship between sleep patterns and injury susceptibility (based on Clemente et al., 2021).
- **Variability in Athletes:** Acknowledging that optimal sleep varies by age, training intensity, and individual needs (based on Kong et al., 2025).

## ✨ Key Features
- **📊 Smart Dashboard:** Input your sleep hours, quality, and training intensity to get an instant recovery status.
- **🧠 Science-Based Advice:** Receive personalized guidance (e.g., "Focus on light recovery today") based on your data.
- **📚 Research Library:** A dedicated section to explore the scientific evidence behind the app's algorithms.
- **🙏 Philosophical Foundation:** Reflecting on the importance of stewardship and taking care of the body as a gift.
